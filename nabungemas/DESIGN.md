# NabungEmas App Design System

## Color Palette
- **Gold400 (Primary):** #F5B93A
- **Gold300 (Gradient Pair):** #F7CA6A
- **Gold200:** #FAE0A8
- **Gold100:** #FEF5E1
- **Neutral900 (Text Dark):** #1A1814
- **Neutral800 (Surface Dark):** #2C2A25
- **Neutral200 (Divider):** #EDEBE6
- **Neutral100 (Background Light):** #F8F6F2
- **Neutral050 (Card Light):** #FDFCFA
- **Success:** #22C55E
- **Error:** #EF4444

## Typography (Plus Jakarta Sans)
- **Display/Large:** 32sp Bold
- **Headline/Large:** 24sp SemiBold
- **Title/Large:** 18sp SemiBold
- **Body/Large:** 16sp Regular
- **Label/Medium:** 12sp Medium

## Spacing & Shapes
- **Base Grid:** 8dp
- **Page Padding:** 20dp
- **Card Radius:** 16dp
- **Button Radius:** 28dp (Pill)
- **Dialog Radius:** 24dp

## Components
- **GoldButton:** Gradient #F5B93A to #F7CA6A, white text, pill shape, 52dp height.
- **GoldProgressBar:** 8dp height, gold gradient fill, rounded full.
- **SavingCard:** white/dark card, 16dp radius, soft shadow 0 4dp 12dp rgba(0,0,0,0.08).
- **BottomNav:** 4 tabs (Home, Saving, Price, About), height 64dp, gold active indicator pill.
- **FAB:** Gold gradient, circle 56dp or extended pill.
- **TextField:** Outlined style, 12dp radius, gold focus border, 52dp height.
