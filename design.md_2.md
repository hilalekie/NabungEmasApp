Design system for "NabungEmas" — an Android gold savings management app.
STYLE: Modern, clean, warm-bright. Inspired by Material Design 3. Eye-friendly with gold accent.
COLOR PALETTE:
- Primary accent: #F5B93A (Gold400), gradient pair: #F7CA6A (Gold300)
- Subtle tint: #FEF5E1 (Gold100)
- Page background: #F8F6F2 (light), #1A1814 (dark)
- Card background: #FDFCFA (light), #2C2A25 (dark)
- Primary text: #1A1814 (light), #F8F6F2 (dark)
- Success: #22C55E, Error: #EF4444, Info: #3B82F6
- Divider/skeleton: #EDEBE6
TYPOGRAPHY: Plus Jakarta Sans
- Display heading (32sp, Bold) for hero numbers
- Headline (24sp, SemiBold) for section titles
- Body (16sp, Regular) for content
- Label (12sp, Medium) for chips and badges
SHAPES: Rounded corners — cards 16dp, buttons 28dp (pill), sheets 20dp
SPACING: 8dp grid system, page horizontal padding 20dp
SHADOWS: Soft — 0 4dp 12dp rgba(0,0,0,0.08) for cards
COMPONENTS:
- GoldButton: gradient #F5B93A→#F7CA6A, white text, pill shape, 52dp height
- GoldProgressBar: 8dp height, gradient fill, rounded full, with % label
- SavingCard: white/dark card, 16dp radius, gold progress bar inside
- BottomNav: 4 tabs (Home, Saving, Price, About), gold active indicator pill
- FAB: gold gradient, circle or extended pill
- TextField: outlined style, 16dp radius, gold focused border
All screens must support Light Mode and Dark Mode.
